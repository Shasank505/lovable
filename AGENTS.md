Year 1 uploaded photos are served through Lovable Assets JSON pointers; this keeps personal image binaries out of source control while preserving a stable ordered gallery.
Chapter progression is kept in page state and reveals one chapter at a time after a kiss animation; this preserves the storybook's sequential reading experience without storing a permanent reading position.
